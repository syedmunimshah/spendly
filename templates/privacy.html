Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the app, we store the expense records, categories, amounts, and dates you enter.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate and improve Spendly — to authenticate your account, display your expense history, and generate the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app.

  • To provide and maintain your account
  • To show you your own spending data and trends
  • To troubleshoot issues and keep the service running reliably

We do not sell your personal information or expense data to third parties, and it is not shared with advertisers or other users.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Spendly's database and protected using reasonable technical safeguards. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You can update or delete the expense records you've entered at any time from within the app. If you wish to delete your account entirely, contact us and we will remove your data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal information or expense data with third-party advertisers. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) solely to operate the app, and any such providers are bound to handle data only as needed to provide that infrastructure.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you'd like to request deletion of your account and data, please reach out to us through the contact details provided in the app.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Spendly after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
